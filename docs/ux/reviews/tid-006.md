Reviewed against `docs/ux/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`, `docs/ux/FLOWS.md` and `docs/ux/screens/app-shell.md`.
Build: branch `tid-006-app-shell` (reviewed on `tid-006-ux-review`), built SPA served by `wrangler dev` on port 8798.

- Capture script: `docs/ux/reviews/capture-tid-006.mjs`. Raw measurements: `docs/ux/reviews/tid-006-measurements.json`.

## Verdict

No blockers. Two majors, both at large text sizes (header title, drawer footer, toast). Everything else in the shell matches the design. Recommend accepting TID-006 once the two majors are fixed, or accepting with the majors logged as defects against the screen stories that first fill these slots (the fixes are small and live in the shell).

## Findings

### Major

**M1. Header title is cut to a few letters at 200% text.** Screenshot: `zoom200-subpage-light.png`, `zoom200-subpage-dark.png`.

- Design: app-shell.md 2.1 and section 7: "At large text sizes it may wrap to 2 lines (`line-clamp-2`)", "Header grows, title may wrap".
- Built: `h1` is `truncate`, so "Import from OPML" renders as "Import fro..." (header 113 px tall, title still one line). The user cannot tell which screen they are on.
- Fix in `apps/web/src/shell/AppShell.tsx`: replace `truncate` on the `h1` with `line-clamp-2 break-words` (keep `min-w-0 flex-1`). Add `py-2` to the row's flex container so a two-line title does not touch the border. At normal sizes the title is still one line, so nothing changes at 375 px.

**M2. Drawer footer overflows at 200% text: "Settings" is clipped off the right edge.** Screenshot: `zoom200-drawer-light.png`, `zoom200-drawer-dark.png`.

- Design: app-shell.md 2.2 "two secondary buttons ... half width" and 11 NFR-9 "Rows grow, no horizontal scroll".
- Built: the Manage/Settings row is `flex gap-2` with `px-2` and `gap-2` inside; at 200% each button's min content is wider than half of 320 px, so the second button runs past the drawer edge.
- Fix in `apps/web/src/shell/FeedNav.tsx`: on the Manage/Settings row use `flex flex-wrap gap-2`, and on each button use `min-w-fit flex-1` (replace `px-2`/`flex-1` as needed). At 200% the buttons then stack as two full-width rows (each still at least 44 high); at normal size they stay side by side.

**M3. Toast message is truncated and clipped at 200% text.** Screenshot: `toast-undo-bar-zoom200-light.png`, `toast-undo-bar-zoom200-dark.png`.

- Design: app-shell.md 2.4 "Message ... up to 2 lines then ellipsis".
- Built: with the Undo button taking about 40% of the width, "Marked 14 as read" becomes "Marked 14 as..." and the third line's descenders peek out under the clamp (`py-2` is inside the clamped `p`). The message is the content the user needs to read before deciding to Undo.
- Fix in `apps/web/src/shell/Toasts.tsx`: make the card `flex flex-wrap items-center` and give the message wrapper `min-w-[12ch] flex-1`, so the action drops to its own row when the message needs the width. Move the vertical padding off the clamped element: wrap it as `<div class="py-2 min-w-0 flex-1"><p class="line-clamp-2">...</p></div>` so the clamp has no padding to leak through. Spec for the message stays "2 lines".

### Minor

**m1. Desktop: the header title does not align with the content column.** Screenshot: `all-d-light.png`, `all-d-dark.png`.

- Design: app-shell.md section 5 "the title is left aligned to the content column".
- Built: title sits at the main-area gutter (x = 304) while the content column is centred at x = 448.
- Fix: at `lg` the header row gets the same wrapper as main: `lg:mx-auto lg:max-w-2xl` on the inner `div.flex.min-h-14`. Keep the header background full width. Do not apply below 1024 px (Menu must stay at the gutter, spec section 4). Right actions then also align to the column edge, which matches the bottom bar.

**m2. The toast offset is a constant (`3.5rem`) while the bar can be taller.** `slots.tsx` sets `--bottom-offset: 3.5rem`. The bar is `min-h-14` plus a 1 px border, so the gap is 11 px, not 12 (cosmetic). If a screen's bar content wraps at large text and grows past 56 px, the toast would overlap it. Fix: in `BottomBar`, set the variable from the bar element's measured `offsetHeight` with a `ResizeObserver`, minus the safe-area inset (the toast formula already adds it). Not visible until a screen supplies a taller bar, so safe to log for the first story that does.

**m3. Reduced motion uses 0.01 ms rather than `none`.** This is what design system section 7 specified and it is visually instant. No action.

### Notes (no change required)

- The drawer empty state repeats "Add a feed" as a link and the footer "Add feed" button. This is what the spec asks for; it disappears once feeds exist.
- Auth card: wordmark "Tidings" above an `h1`; for `/setup` the h1 "Set up Tidings" repeats the wordmark. Acceptable (see placeholder answers).

## Engineer's deviations

1. **No swipe-to-close: accept.** The spec marks it as an enhancement, not an acceptance requirement, and there are four other ways to close (scrim, Close, Escape, system Back), all verified. Swipe is also the riskiest gesture to get right on iOS (it competes with the Safari back gesture). Carry it as a backlog nice-to-have; do not block on it.
2. **Bottom bar is in-flow `sticky bottom-0`, not fixed: accept, and I prefer it.** Behaviour verified with an injected bar: with short content `main` is `flex-1` so the bar sits at the bottom of the viewport; with long content it sticks to the bottom. Content is never hidden behind it, so no padding hack is needed, and the home-indicator inset is handled by the bar's own padding. It also removes the iOS problem where `fixed` bars jump while the browser toolbar collapses. Update app-shell.md section 6 ("Main bottom padding equals the bar height") to say the bar is in flow. The one cost is finding m2.
3. **Header title truncates to one line instead of wrapping at large text: reject.** This is finding M1. Truncation is right at normal size and wrong at large sizes, so keep `truncate` behaviour only for the single line case by using `line-clamp-2`.
4. **Offline banner driven by the browser `offline` event only: accept for TID-006, with a condition.** There are no API calls in this build, so a failed-request trigger has nothing to hook into, and the first-load state is correct because the hook reads `navigator.onLine`. The condition: the data-layer story (TID-018 or whichever adds the query client) must add the second trigger the spec requires (a network failure with no response sets the offline state; the next success clears it), because phones often report `onLine = true` on captive portals and weak signal. Add this as an acceptance line on that story.

**Placeholder titles**

| Route | Built | Recommendation |

| `/login` | Sign in | Accept (already in FLOWS 2.1). |
| `/recover` | Use a recovery code | Accept. Names the one thing the screen does. |

| `/feeds/:id` and article routes | Feed | Same as Folder. Skeleton in the header while loading; custom title if set. |

Add these four to the "Header title per route" table in app-shell.md as the loading/fallback titles.

**Header Back rules**

- **Article: one entry back if the app has history, otherwise its list route.** Agree, and it matches FLOWS 1.6. Verified: `/feeds/s1/a/e1` opened directly goes to `/feeds/s1`. Two notes. First, the built parent is the list in the route path (`/all`, `/folders/:id` or `/feeds/:id`), not "the feed the article belongs to" as FLOWS 1.6 says. That is better (it returns to the list the user was reading, and needs no data), so FLOWS should change to match. Second, next/previous inside an article must use `replace` so "one entry back" is always the list.
- **Add and Import go to `/manage`: accept as the fallback, but I recommend the article rule for them too.** Use "one entry back if the app has history, otherwise `/manage`". Reasoning: a new user on an empty `/all` taps "Add feed" in the drawer, adds a feed and presses Back. They expect to return to `/all` to see it, not to land on a Manage screen they never opened. With the rule as built, Back also pushes a new entry, so system Back then returns into the form. If the PM wants the simpler fixed rule, then at least make the header Back use `replace: true` so history does not grow. Either way, update the Add feed, Import row in FLOWS 1.6.

## Fixes

Branch `tid-006-ux-fixes` (from `tid-006-app-shell`). Regression tests: `e2e/specs/tid-006-large-text.spec.ts` (each fails on the pre-fix code). Before images are in `docs/ux/reviews/tid-006/before-fix/`; the images next to them are the after images.

- **M1 (`AppShell.tsx`).** The `h1` is now `line-clamp-2 break-words` instead of `truncate`, and the header row has `py-2`. At 200% text "Import from OPML" wraps to two lines and the header grows; at normal size nothing changes. After: `zoom200-subpage-light.png`, `zoom200-subpage-dark.png`.
- **M2 (`FeedNav.tsx`).** The Manage/Settings row is `flex flex-wrap` and each link has `min-w-fit flex-1`. At 200% text they stack as two full-width rows inside the drawer (both 44 px or more high); at normal size they stay side by side. After: `zoom200-drawer-light.png`, `zoom200-drawer-dark.png`.
- **M3 (`Toasts.tsx`).** The card is `flex flex-wrap items-center gap-x-2`. Padding moved off the clamped `p` onto a wrapper `div.min-w-[12ch].flex-1.py-2`, so nothing leaks under the clamp, and the Undo/Dismiss button has `ml-auto`. At 200% text "Marked 14 as read" shows in full and Undo drops to its own row, right aligned. At normal size the message and the button stay on one row (checked with `toast-undo-bar-*` and `toast-error-bar-*`). After: `toast-undo-bar-zoom200-light.png`, `toast-undo-bar-zoom200-dark.png`.
- **m1 (`AppShell.tsx`).** The header's inner row has `lg:mx-auto lg:max-w-2xl`. `max-w-2xl` (42 rem) is the width of the content column in `main`, and the header and `main` have identical side padding, so the title's left edge equals the column's (448 px at 1280). The header background still spans the full main area. Below 1024 px nothing changes. After: `all-d-light.png`, `all-d-dark.png` (before: `before-fix/all-d-*.png`).
- **m2 (`slots.tsx`).** `BottomBar` now sets `--bottom-offset` to the bar's measured height (minus the bar's own safe-area padding, which the toast formula adds itself) using a `ResizeObserver`, and removes the property and disconnects on unmount. The gap is exactly 12 px, and it follows the bar if it grows.
